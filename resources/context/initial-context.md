## Objective
We need to develop a Dashboard to optimize decision-making and tracking for the ver+ Beca Tech program using tech tools. This is the KR FT1.3 Scholars Progress Dashboard used in decision. 
## Context
- Currently that data is scattered across forms and spreadsheets, which makes reporting manual and slow.
- The dashboard will have two layers:
	- The most urgent one is: Scholar’s tracking.
	- The second one is: Pipeline of Selection process

### How the data works today for the Scholar’s tracking Layer
#### Our current setup is a simple two-stage flow:
#### Collection - JotForm. Online forms gather information from scholars and mentors:
- Scholar wellbeing check-in — scholars report on their own wellbeing: Colombia and Peru
- Mentor reports — mentors report on each scholar's status:
- Scholar requests — requests submitted by scholars to the program:

#### Consolidation- Excel (master file). A master spreadsheet pulls the form data together and organizes it into tables and reports. It holds information from both the selection and scholar-support processes — personal, academic, and psychosocial data, among others — integrated from the mentor and scholar forms. It is organized into tabs:

##### OVERALL MONITORING: Main dashboard with key program indicators: level of participation in activities and risk status of the scholarship recipients. This is the recommended entry point for executive review.

##### SCHOLAR HISTORY: Search for individual information on each scholar. 

##### SCHOLAR GENERAL INFO: Complete view of each recipient: current status, sociodemographic data, academic progress, and record of support activities from year 1 to 5.

### We need a Dashboard to visualize  the previous data for decision making to answer these questions: 
- Total active scholars. Filter by cohort, country, and university. Gender, Department.
- Total Scholars withdraw. Filter by cohort, country, and university, last semester.
- Average program retention rate. Retention filter by cohort, country, and university, last semester.
- Average program satisfaction rate. Filter by cohort, country, and university, last semester.
- Average scholar GPA overall. Filter by cohort, country, and university.
- Scholar risk level: no risk, low, medium, high, critical. Filter by cohort, university, and country.
- Scholar participation in support activities. Filter by cohort, university, and country.
- Monthly changes in scholar risk level. Filter by cohort, country, and university.
- Alert type: psychosocial or academic, for medium, high, and critical risk scholars. Filter by cohort, country, and university.
- Review whether scholars are progressing as expected in the program. Filter by cohort, country, and university.
- Visualize the program's unit economics.
